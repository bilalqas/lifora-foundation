A clear process for refund requests.

This policy explains how LIFORA Foundation handles refund requests involving duplicate payments, payment mistakes, potentially unauthorized transactions, bank transfers, card payments, and cryptocurrency contributions.

🧾

Contributions are generally final, but mistakes can happen.

Certain requests may be reviewed depending on the payment method, timing, circumstances, whether funds remain available, and the rules of the relevant payment provider or financial institution.

1. Overview

Contributions made to LIFORA Foundation are generally intended to be final. However, we recognize that payment mistakes, accidental duplicates, or potentially unauthorized transactions can occur.

Where appropriate, LIFORA may review a refund request on a case-by-case basis.

Approval is not automatic and may depend on the payment method, timing of the request, whether the funds remain available, third-party provider rules, and applicable law.

2. Refund Requests We May Review

LIFORA may review requests involving circumstances such as:

  • an accidental duplicate payment;
  • an incorrect donation amount entered by mistake;
  • a transaction that the account holder believes was unauthorized;
  • a technical or payment-processing error;
  • a payment sent to LIFORA in error; or
  • another exceptional circumstance that reasonably warrants review.

A request being eligible for review does not guarantee that a refund will be approved.

3. When to Submit a Refund Request

If you believe a payment was made in error, contact LIFORA as soon as reasonably possible after discovering the issue.

Recommended timing

For the best chance of review before funds are transferred, converted, committed, or used, please contact us promptly after the transaction.

Delays can reduce the possibility of a refund, particularly for bank transfers, cryptocurrency, currency conversion, or funds that have already been used for humanitarian or operational purposes.

4. Card Payment Refunds

Card payments are not currently active on the LIFORA website. If card payments are enabled in the future, they will be handled through an established third-party payment provider.

Refunds for card transactions may be subject to:

  • the payment provider's refund rules;
  • card-network requirements;
  • fraud or dispute review;
  • processing timelines; and
  • any non-refundable processing charges.

A card refund, if approved, would normally be returned through the original payment method where technically and legally possible.

5. Bank Transfer Refunds

International bank transfers can involve sending-bank fees, intermediary-bank fees, receiving-bank fees, currency conversion, and correspondent banking.

Current bank-account disclosure

The receiving account currently displayed on LIFORA's donation page is held in the personal name Muhammad Bilal Qasim while LIFORA completes its organizational development and registration.

If a bank-transfer refund is approved, the donor may be responsible for bank, intermediary, or transfer fees required to return the funds.

The amount returned may therefore be lower than the amount originally sent.

6. Cryptocurrency Refunds

Cryptocurrency transactions are generally irreversible.

Blockchain transfers cannot normally be cancelled once confirmed, and sending cryptocurrency to the wrong address or network may result in permanent loss.

LIFORA cannot guarantee recovery or refund of cryptocurrency sent:

  • to an incorrect wallet address;
  • using the wrong blockchain network;
  • as an unsupported token or asset;
  • without sufficient information to identify the sender; or
  • after the received asset has already been transferred or converted.

If a cryptocurrency refund is exceptionally approved, LIFORA may return the same asset or an equivalent amount determined at the time of refund, subject to operational feasibility, compliance checks, network fees, and applicable law.

Network or transaction fees may be deducted from the amount returned.

7. Funds That Have Already Been Used or Committed

A refund may not be possible where the contribution has already been spent, distributed, transferred, committed to a supplier or partner, converted into another currency or asset, or otherwise used for humanitarian or reasonable operational purposes.

This is one reason donors should contact LIFORA promptly after identifying a payment error.

8. Processing Fees, Currency Conversion and Exchange Rates

Banks, payment providers, card networks, exchanges, blockchain networks, or other intermediaries may charge fees.

Where a refund is approved, LIFORA does not guarantee that third-party fees, network fees, card-processing charges, bank charges, or currency conversion losses can be recovered.

If a payment involved currency or cryptocurrency conversion, the value returned may differ from the value originally contributed because of exchange-rate or market movements.

9. Potentially Unauthorized or Fraudulent Payments

If you believe a card, bank account, wallet, or other payment method was used without authorization, contact the relevant bank, card issuer, wallet provider, or payment provider immediately as well as LIFORA.

LIFORA may request information needed to review the transaction, cooperate with payment providers or financial institutions, and take reasonable fraud-prevention or compliance steps.

10. Refund Review Process

To review a request, LIFORA may ask for information such as:

  • your name and contact information;
  • the transaction date;
  • the amount and currency or cryptocurrency asset;
  • the payment method used;
  • a bank reference, payment-provider reference, or transaction hash;
  • the reason for the request; and
  • reasonable evidence supporting the request.

We may also need to verify that the person requesting the refund is the person who made or is authorized to act in relation to the payment.

Requests involving fraud, sanctions, suspicious activity, or compliance concerns may require additional review.

11. When a Refund May Be Refused

A refund request may be declined where, for example:

  • the payment was knowingly and voluntarily made;
  • the funds have already been used or committed;
  • the transaction cannot technically be reversed;
  • the request cannot be reasonably verified;
  • the request appears fraudulent or abusive;
  • returning funds would violate law, sanctions, or provider rules;
  • the requested refund is inconsistent with the original transaction; or
  • another legitimate reason prevents the refund.

12. Current Organizational and Tax Status

Important disclosure

LIFORA Foundation is currently in organizational development and registration and does not currently represent itself as a registered charity, tax-exempt organization, or U.S. 501(c)(3) organization.

Contributions should not currently be assumed to be tax-deductible.

A payment confirmation or refund decision does not establish any tax-deductible status.

13. Changes to this Refund Policy

LIFORA may update this Refund Policy as payment methods, providers, legal requirements, or operational practices change.

The most recent version will be posted on this page with the updated revision date.

14. Contact Us About a Refund

If you need to request review of a payment, contact LIFORA through the contact information provided on the website and include as much transaction information as reasonably possible.

💚 RESPONSIBLE GIVING

How donations may be used.

LIFORA Foundation aims to use contributions responsibly across humanitarian programs and the reasonable operational needs required to plan, deliver, document and support those activities.

🧾

Clear purpose. Responsible use.

We do not promise that 100% of every contribution goes directly to beneficiaries. Effective humanitarian work can also require payment processing, administration, compliance, logistics and other reasonable operating costs.

Program Uses

Contributions may support these humanitarian areas.

🍲

Food & Essential Relief

Food, clean water, clothing, hygiene items and other essential household or emergency supplies for people facing hardship.

📚

Education & Opportunity

School supplies, books, learning resources, educational assistance and other appropriate support related to learning opportunities.

🏥

Health & Medical Support

Essential medicines, medical supplies, healthcare-related assistance and other appropriate support for health needs.

🆘

Emergency & Disaster Relief

Food, essential supplies, urgent assistance, shelter-related support and other appropriate humanitarian response during emergencies.

Responsible Use of Funds

Humanitarian work also requires operational support.

Program and operational expenses can both be necessary.

LIFORA aims to direct resources toward humanitarian purposes while maintaining the systems needed to operate responsibly.

We therefore do not state that every contribution will be used exclusively for direct beneficiary purchases.

Reasonable expenses may include:

  • Payment-processing and banking fees
  • Currency conversion and transfer costs
  • Transportation and logistics
  • Procurement and distribution costs
  • Website, communication and technology expenses
  • Administrative and record-keeping costs
  • Legal, compliance and registration expenses
  • Accounting and financial-control expenses
  • Other reasonable costs required to support humanitarian activities
Designated Contributions

When a donor chooses a particular cause.

IMPORTANT INFORMATION

We aim to respect donor preferences where reasonably possible.

When a donor selects a specific humanitarian area, LIFORA intends to use the contribution toward that area where reasonably practical.

However, circumstances can change. A project may become fully funded, delayed, impractical, unsafe, legally restricted or otherwise unable to proceed as originally expected.

In those circumstances, LIFORA may use the contribution for another closely related humanitarian need or for the area of greatest need, subject to applicable law and any binding restrictions.

Allocation Principles

How we intend to make responsible decisions.

🎯

Humanitarian Need

Resources should be directed toward legitimate humanitarian purposes based on practical needs and priorities.

📋

Documentation

LIFORA aims to maintain appropriate records for major expenses, projects and activities.

🛡️

Compliance

Funds should not knowingly be used for unlawful, sanctioned, fraudulent or prohibited activities.

🤝

Responsible Partners

Where partners or suppliers are used, LIFORA aims to select suitable parties appropriate to the activity.

🔎

Transparency

As projects are carried out, LIFORA intends to publish meaningful updates and supporting information where appropriate.

🌍

Where Needed Most

Unrestricted contributions may be allocated to humanitarian priorities and operational needs where LIFORA determines support is reasonably needed.

CURRENT ORGANIZATIONAL STATUS

Contributions are not currently represented as tax-deductible charitable donations.

LIFORA Foundation is currently in the organizational development and registration stage and does not currently represent itself as a registered charity, tax-exempt organization, or U.S. 501(c)(3) organization.

Unless and until an applicable tax-exempt status is formally obtained, donors should not assume that contributions are eligible for a tax deduction.

CURRENT DEVELOPMENT STAGE

LIFORA has not yet published verified humanitarian impact figures.

LIFORA Foundation is currently developing its organizational, compliance and operational infrastructure. We are not claiming beneficiary totals, completed projects or country reach that cannot yet be supported by evidence.

Once genuine humanitarian activities are carried out, this page will be updated with verified information such as project dates, locations, resources used, people supported and supporting records.

This approach is intended to protect donor trust and ensure that future impact claims can be reasonably verified.

Evidence & Accountability

What should support an impact claim.

Evidence should match the claim.

If LIFORA reports that an activity happened, we aim to maintain reasonable supporting records showing what was done.

If we publish a number, that number should be based on records that can be explained rather than estimated for marketing purposes.

Future impact reports may include:

  • Project name and purpose
  • Activity date or period
  • Country, city or general location
  • Verified or documented beneficiary information
  • Resources purchased or distributed
  • Major expenditure information
  • Receipts or invoices where appropriate
  • Photos or activity evidence where suitable
  • Partner or supplier information where appropriate
  • Project outcome or completion notes
From Activity to Impact

A simple reporting journey.

1

Plan

Define the humanitarian need, intended response, expected costs and records required.

2

Deliver

Carry out the activity directly or with suitable partners while maintaining appropriate records.

3

Verify

Review activity details, beneficiary information, expenses and supporting evidence.

4

Report

Publish a meaningful summary so supporters can understand what was done and what was achieved.

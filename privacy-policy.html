🔒 PRIVACY POLICY

Your privacy matters.

This Privacy Policy explains what information LIFORA Foundation may receive through its website, donations, communications, analytics, and third-party service providers, and how that information may be used and protected.

🛡️

We aim to collect only what is reasonably needed.

LIFORA does not currently operate its own card-processing system. When card payments are enabled, complete card details will be handled by the applicable payment provider rather than stored directly by this website.

1. Scope of this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to information handled in connection with the LIFORA Foundation website and related communications, donation activity, analytics, and online services.

It does not replace the privacy policies of banks, payment processors, cryptocurrency platforms, wallet providers, hosting companies, or other third parties that may process information independently.

2. Information We May Receive

Depending on how you interact with LIFORA, information may include:

  • your name;
  • email address or other contact information you choose to provide;
  • messages, enquiries, or correspondence you send to LIFORA;
  • donation amount, currency, cause preference, and payment method;
  • transaction references or payment confirmations;
  • bank-transfer reference information;
  • cryptocurrency transaction hashes and public wallet information;
  • technical website information such as IP-related, browser, device, or request data processed by hosting/security providers; and
  • other information you voluntarily provide.

LIFORA does not ask donors to provide passwords, private keys, seed phrases, or recovery phrases.

3. Website and Technical Information

When you visit the website, technical information may be processed automatically by internet infrastructure, hosting, security, analytics, or content-delivery providers.

This may include request information, approximate location derived from network data, device type, browser information, pages visited, referring source, performance information, and security-related signals.

Some of this information may be necessary to deliver the website, protect it from abuse, diagnose problems, understand traffic, and improve performance.

4. Analytics, Hosting and Cloudflare

LIFORA uses Cloudflare infrastructure for services associated with the website, including hosting, DNS, content delivery, security, and analytics tools where enabled.

These services may process technical request and website-usage information in order to provide, protect, analyze, and improve website delivery.

Website statistics

LIFORA may use aggregated or technical website analytics to understand visits, page views, traffic sources, countries, device categories, and website performance.

5. Donation and Contribution Information

If you make a contribution, LIFORA may receive or maintain information needed to identify, reconcile, document, or respond to the transaction.

This may include the contribution amount, currency, date, payment method, cause preference, transaction reference, payment-provider information, or information provided with a bank transfer.

Donation information may be used for accounting, record keeping, fraud prevention, compliance, supporter service, dispute handling, and internal reporting.

6. Card Payments

Card payments are not currently active on the LIFORA website.

When card payments are enabled, LIFORA intends to use an established third-party payment provider so that complete card details are submitted to and processed by that provider rather than stored directly by LIFORA's website.

Complete card numbers

LIFORA does not currently collect or store complete card numbers, card security codes, or online-banking passwords directly on this website.

The payment provider may independently collect identity, transaction, fraud-prevention, device, and payment information under its own privacy terms.

7. Bank Transfers

When you make a bank transfer, banks and financial institutions involved in the transaction may process personal and financial information independently.

LIFORA may receive information displayed on the incoming payment, such as the sender name, payment reference, amount, currency, sending institution, or other information transmitted through the banking system.

LIFORA does not control what information your bank or intermediary financial institutions collect or retain.

8. Cryptocurrency Contributions

Public blockchains are generally transparent. A cryptocurrency transaction may permanently record information such as the sending address, receiving address, transaction hash, asset, amount, time, and network.

Blockchain records may remain publicly accessible even if LIFORA removes information from its own systems.

If you use an exchange, wallet provider, or other cryptocurrency service, that provider may separately collect and process personal information under its own policies.

9. How Information May Be Used

LIFORA may use information for purposes such as:

  • operating and maintaining the website;
  • responding to enquiries and supporter communications;
  • processing, reconciling, and documenting contributions;
  • reviewing refund or transaction issues;
  • fraud prevention and security;
  • accounting, record keeping, and financial controls;
  • legal, regulatory, banking, and compliance requirements;
  • website analytics and performance improvement;
  • investigating misuse or suspicious activity;
  • protecting LIFORA, supporters, beneficiaries, and service providers; and
  • other purposes reasonably related to the interaction for which information was provided.

10. Sharing with Service Providers and Other Parties

LIFORA may need to share or allow access to information with third parties that provide services or are involved in a transaction.

These may include:

  • website hosting, DNS, security, and analytics providers;
  • payment processors and card networks when card payments are enabled;
  • banks and financial institutions;
  • accountants, professional advisers, or compliance service providers;
  • technology and communications providers;
  • fraud-prevention or security providers;
  • government, regulatory, law-enforcement, or legal authorities where required or appropriate; and
  • other parties where reasonably necessary to complete a transaction or protect legal rights.

Third parties may process information under their own privacy notices and legal obligations.

11. Sale of Personal Information

LIFORA does not sell personal information to advertisers or data brokers for monetary consideration.

Information may still be processed by service providers where necessary to operate the website, process transactions, provide security, comply with law, or perform the functions described in this policy.

12. Information Security

LIFORA aims to use reasonable technical and organizational measures appropriate to its current size, systems, and activities.

However, no internet transmission, email, website, bank transfer, blockchain transaction, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You should never send LIFORA passwords, private keys, seed phrases, recovery phrases, or other secret authentication credentials.

13. Data Retention

LIFORA may retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including transaction records, accounting, dispute handling, fraud prevention, compliance, security, and legal obligations.

Different categories of information may be retained for different periods depending on operational and legal needs.

Public blockchain information is not controlled by LIFORA and may remain permanently available on the applicable blockchain.

14. International Processing

LIFORA's website may be accessed internationally, and service providers may process information in countries different from your own.

By using global hosting, banking, payment, analytics, or cryptocurrency infrastructure, information may be transmitted across national borders subject to the laws and safeguards applicable to the relevant service provider.

15. Privacy Questions and Requests

Depending on where you live and applicable law, you may have certain rights relating to personal information, such as requesting access, correction, deletion, or information about processing.

These rights are not absolute and may be limited by legal, accounting, fraud-prevention, security, transaction-record, or compliance requirements.

You may contact LIFORA through the website to make a privacy enquiry.

16. Children's Privacy

LIFORA's donation and general website services are not designed to knowingly collect sensitive personal information from children without appropriate involvement from a parent, guardian, or other legally authorized person where required.

If you believe a child has provided personal information in a way that should be reviewed, please contact LIFORA.

17. External Websites and Third-Party Services

The website may contain links to banks, payment providers, cryptocurrency services, social platforms, or other third-party websites.

LIFORA is not responsible for the privacy practices of those independent services. You should review the relevant provider's privacy information before submitting personal or payment data.

18. Changes to this Privacy Policy

LIFORA may update this Privacy Policy as the website, payment methods, service providers, organizational structure, or legal requirements change.

The current version will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

19. Contact

For privacy-related questions or requests, please contact LIFORA using the contact information provided on the website.

1. Acceptance of these Donation Terms

These Donation Terms apply to contributions made through or in connection with the LIFORA Foundation website.

By making a contribution, you confirm that you have read and understood these terms and agree that your contribution will be handled subject to them and to applicable law.

You should also review our How Donations Are Used, Refund Policy, and Privacy Policy.

2. Current Organizational and Tax Status

Important organizational-status disclosure

LIFORA Foundation is currently in organizational development and registration. It does not currently represent itself as a registered charity, tax-exempt organization, or U.S. 501(c)(3) organization.

Unless and until an applicable tax-exempt status is formally obtained, contributions should not be assumed to be tax-deductible.

The use of the word “Foundation” in the LIFORA name does not by itself mean that LIFORA has charitable or tax-exempt legal status.

3. Voluntary Contributions

Contributions are voluntary. You are responsible for deciding whether, how much, and by which available method you wish to give.

You confirm that any contribution you make is from funds that you are legally entitled to use and that you are authorized to make the transaction.

4. Designated Giving and Cause Selection

Our donation page may allow you to select a particular cause, such as Food & Essential Relief, Education & Opportunity, Health & Medical Support, Emergency & Disaster Relief, or “Where Needed Most.”

LIFORA intends to respect a donor's stated preference where reasonably practical. However, a project or program may become fully funded, delayed, unsafe, impractical, legally restricted, unavailable, or otherwise unable to proceed as expected.

In those circumstances, and subject to applicable law and any binding restriction, LIFORA may redirect the contribution to a closely related humanitarian purpose or to an area of greatest need.

More information is available on our How Donations Are Used page.

5. Payment Methods

LIFORA may make one or more contribution methods available, including card payments, international bank transfers, and cryptocurrency.

Availability can change. A method shown on the website may be inactive, limited, under review, or available only through a third-party provider.

Card payments

Card payments will only be accepted after an appropriate payment provider has been connected and enabled. LIFORA's website does not currently collect or store complete card numbers directly.

When card processing is enabled, the applicable payment provider may apply its own terms, verification procedures, fraud controls, processing rules, and fees.

6. International Bank Transfers

Personal receiving-account disclosure

The bank receiving account currently shown on LIFORA's donation page is held in the personal name Muhammad Bilal Qasim while LIFORA completes its organizational development and registration. It is not currently represented as a registered-charity bank account or tax-deductible donation account.

You are responsible for checking all transfer details before authorizing a bank payment, including the account name, bank, IBAN, SWIFT/BIC, currency, and any correspondent-bank details required by your sending bank.

Your bank or intermediary banks may deduct fees from the amount sent or received. LIFORA is not responsible for fees charged by your bank, intermediary banks, or other financial institutions.

7. Cryptocurrency Contributions

Cryptocurrency transactions can be irreversible. Before sending cryptocurrency, you are responsible for checking both the wallet address and the correct blockchain network.

  • Do not send an asset using an unsupported or incorrect network.
  • Do not rely only on the visual appearance of an address; verify the full public address shown on the donation page.
  • Network, exchange, or wallet fees may reduce the amount received.
  • Blockchain transactions generally cannot be cancelled or reversed after confirmation.
Tax status of crypto contributions

LIFORA does not currently represent cryptocurrency contributions as tax-deductible donations.

8. Processing Fees, Exchange Rates and Currency Conversion

Third-party providers, banks, card networks, cryptocurrency platforms, wallets, or exchanges may charge transaction, processing, network, conversion, or intermediary fees.

Where a contribution is converted from one currency or asset to another, the final value received may differ because of exchange rates, market movement, provider spreads, or fees.

Unless explicitly stated otherwise, LIFORA does not guarantee that the full amount initiated by the donor will be received after third-party charges.

9. Monthly Giving and Recurring Contributions

The website may allow a visitor to indicate a preference for “Monthly” giving. A recurring payment is not created merely by selecting that preference.

Recurring billing only becomes active when an enabled payment provider clearly confirms a recurring-payment arrangement and the donor authorizes it.

Any cancellation process for a future recurring card payment will depend on the payment provider and any instructions displayed at the time recurring payments are enabled.

10. Refunds, Mistakes and Unauthorized Transactions

Contributions are generally intended to be final, but certain situations may be reviewed, including an accidental duplicate, an incorrect amount, or a potentially unauthorized transaction.

Refund eligibility depends on the circumstances, the payment method, whether funds have already been used or transferred, provider rules, and applicable law.

Cryptocurrency and some bank transfers may be impossible to reverse.

Please review the complete Refund Policy .

11. Donor Responsibilities

By making a contribution, you confirm that:

  • you are legally permitted to make the contribution;
  • you are using funds, accounts, cards, wallets, or payment instruments that you are authorized to use;
  • information you provide is accurate to the best of your knowledge;
  • your contribution is not made for an unlawful purpose; and
  • you understand the specific risks of the payment method you choose.

12. Fraud, Sanctions and Prohibited Activity

LIFORA may decline, hold, return, review, or report a transaction where reasonably necessary to comply with applicable law, financial institution requirements, sanctions obligations, anti-fraud controls, anti-money-laundering requirements, or payment-provider rules.

A contribution must not be used to facilitate money laundering, sanctions evasion, fraud, terrorism financing, or any other unlawful activity.

13. Receipts, Confirmations and Records

A payment confirmation, blockchain record, bank receipt, or payment-provider receipt confirms a transaction but does not by itself establish that a contribution is tax-deductible.

LIFORA may maintain transaction and donor records for accounting, fraud prevention, reporting, compliance, dispute handling, and operational purposes, subject to our Privacy Policy .

14. Changes to these Donation Terms

LIFORA may update these Donation Terms as payment methods, organizational arrangements, legal requirements, policies, or operational practices change.

The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised “Last updated” date. Terms in effect at the time of a contribution will generally govern that contribution, subject to applicable law.

15. Questions and Contact

If you have a question about a contribution, transaction, refund request, or these Donation Terms, please contact LIFORA through the contact information provided on the website.

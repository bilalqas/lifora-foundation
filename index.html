OUR HUMANITARIAN WORK

Helping people where support matters most.

LIFORA Foundation aims to support vulnerable individuals, families and communities through practical humanitarian action.

🍲

Food & Essential Relief

Supporting vulnerable families and communities with food, essential supplies and basic necessities during times of hardship.

📚

Education & Opportunity

Helping children and young people access education, school supplies, learning opportunities and a brighter future.

🏥

Health & Medical Support

Helping vulnerable people access essential healthcare, medicines and medical support when assistance is needed.

🆘

Responding to emergencies and disasters with food, essential supplies, shelter assistance and humanitarian support.

🤝 OUR WORK & TRANSPARENCY

Compassion should be matched by accountability.

LIFORA Foundation is developing humanitarian programs focused on essential relief, education, healthcare and emergency assistance. As activities are carried out, we intend to publish clear and verifiable information about what was done and how support was used.

🍲

Food & Essential Relief

📚

Education & Opportunity

How We Intend to Work

A simple framework for responsible humanitarian activity.

1

Identify a Need

Understand the humanitarian need, location, intended beneficiaries and practical requirements before committing resources.

2

Plan the Response

Define the purpose, expected costs, delivery method and records needed to support responsible implementation.

3

Carry Out the Activity

Deliver assistance directly or through suitable partners while maintaining appropriate documentation.

4

Report & Review

Publish meaningful updates and review supporting records so supporters can understand how activities were carried out.

Transparency

What we intend to publish for completed activities.

The exact records available may differ by project, location, safety considerations and privacy requirements.

Transparency is part of the work.

Humanitarian organizations should be able to explain what they did, why they did it and how resources were used.

LIFORA intends to build its reporting processes around clear, understandable and verifiable information.

Project records may include:

  • Project or activity name
  • Country, city or general location
  • Date or activity period
  • Purpose and type of assistance
  • Amounts spent or resources distributed
  • Beneficiary information in a privacy-respecting form
  • Receipts, invoices or procurement records where appropriate
  • Photographs or activity evidence where consent and safety allow
  • Partner or supplier information where appropriate
  • Completion notes and lessons learned

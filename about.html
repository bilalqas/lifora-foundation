Our Story

Built around dignity, hope and opportunity.

LIFORA was created with the intention of bringing people together to support individuals and communities facing hardship.

Why LIFORA exists

Around the world, people can face hunger, limited access to education, medical difficulties and sudden emergencies. LIFORA aims to create a responsible platform through which supporters can contribute toward humanitarian initiatives designed to address those needs.

What we are building

We are developing the organizational, governance, compliance and operational foundations needed to carry out humanitarian activities responsibly. As programs are launched, LIFORA intends to publish clear updates and supporting information about its activities.

Mission & Vision

Helping people move from hardship toward possibility.

🎯

Our Mission

To support vulnerable people and communities through responsible humanitarian initiatives focused on essential needs, education, health and emergency relief.

🌱

Our Vision

A world in which more people have the support, resources and opportunities they need to live with dignity, hope and possibility.

What We Focus On

Humanitarian support where it can matter most.

LIFORA is developing programs around four core areas of humanitarian need.

🍲

Food & Essential Relief

Supporting access to food, clean water and essential supplies for people and families facing difficult circumstances.

📚

Education & Opportunity

Helping expand access to learning resources, school supplies and educational opportunities for children and young people.

🏥

Health & Medical Support

Supporting access to essential medical assistance, medicines and healthcare-related needs where practical and appropriate.

🆘

Emergency & Disaster Relief

Supporting humanitarian response during emergencies with essential supplies, food and other urgent assistance.

Our Principles

Trust must be earned through responsible action.

Human Dignity

People receiving support should be treated with respect, compassion and dignity.

Responsible Giving

Contributions should be handled carefully and used for legitimate humanitarian and operational purposes.

Transparency

As activities are carried out, LIFORA intends to publish meaningful updates and supporting information where appropriate.

Accountability

Clear processes, documentation and internal controls are important parts of responsible humanitarian work.

Non-Discrimination

Humanitarian assistance should be guided by need and delivered without unfair discrimination.

Long-Term Responsibility

We aim to build systems that can support sustainable, compliant and effective humanitarian activity over time.

Current Organizational Status

We believe visitors and supporters should clearly understand LIFORA's current stage of development.

LIFORA is currently being developed and organized.

LIFORA Foundation is currently in the organizational development and registration stage. We are building the governance, compliance and operational framework needed to support humanitarian initiatives responsibly.

LIFORA does not currently represent itself as a registered charity, tax-exempt organization, or U.S. 501(c)(3) organization.

Unless and until an applicable tax-exempt status is formally obtained, contributions should not be assumed to be tax-deductible.
